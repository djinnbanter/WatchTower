# Surface brief: web/marketing (Persuade)

## Scope and visitor mode
Public marketing site for WatchTower. Mode: **Persuade**. Expand Night Watch Desk from DESIGN.md — do not replace the design system.

## Audience, job, action
- Audience: skeptical Minecraft dedicated-server admins (solo / small team).
- Job: understand what WatchTower is, how it works, and whether to install it.
- Primary actions: Get it on Modrinth; Try the demo; Install.

## Proof / content
Real screenshots from docs/assets/screenshots/; interactive demo of the real dashboard with baked fixtures; verbatim Promises and Not our job from docs/ROADMAP.md.

## Constraints
Product truth only; WatchTower spelling; no AI-SaaS chrome; no testimonials; no hardcoded versions; dark-only v1; motion budget of three; WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Direction
First viewport: lantern + headline + one support line + CTA pair + Overview screenshot. Signature moment: Watching → Scanning → Fix inbox signal line.

## Memorable moment
Clicking into a live demo of the real ops desk before downloading anything.
