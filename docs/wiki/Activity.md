# Activity

**Activity** is a timeline of commands, joins, lag, and jobs from the latest ops scan — useful for “what happened around then?”

---

## When to open it

- Overview’s incident story needs more detail
- Correlating a lag window with joins/commands
- Checking whether background jobs ran

---

## Activity vs Insights vs Issues

| Tab | Job |
|-----|-----|
| **Activity** | Event timeline / incident story |
| **Insights** | Patterns over days/weeks |
| **Issues** | Fix inbox to act on |

---

## What you’ll see

- KPI summary for the recent window
- Typed timeline (filter chips by event kind)
- Incident story deep links into related tabs

Events come from **Scanning** (and gap backfill). Freshness: [[Sources]].

---

## What to do next

1. Filter to the event types you care about
2. Follow deep links into [[Crashes]], [[Mods]], or [[Backups]]
3. If the timeline is thin, wait for Scanning or click Refresh
